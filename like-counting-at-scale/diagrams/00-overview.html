Like Toggle, Plain Sight

This is the entire user-facing feature: tapping the heart flips one boolean for this (user, post) pair, and the count beside it moves by exactly one. Every mechanism in the rest of this module — sharded counters, write-behind queues, eventual consistency — exists only to make that one number update instantly and correctly at a billion-like scale.
